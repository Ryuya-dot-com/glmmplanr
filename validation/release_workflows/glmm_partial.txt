### Sample Size Justification

No evaluated design has confirmed attainment within constraints. The candidate is 22 participants x 12 items (fail, 6 replications). Joint detection-and-width attainment was 33.3% [9.7%, 70.0%], and mean accuracy difference interval width was 30.58 percentage points (1.612 logit). Final verification covered 15 of 15 lattice points. The same-replication target is 80%. Width limit 30.00 percentage points, width attainment 50.0%, same-replication detection and width attainment 33.3% (MC interval 9.7 to 70.0%). Primary analysis: bayes_laplace, uncorrelated random effects, logit link. Target uncertainty: parametric bootstrap (99 refits per successful replication). The target interval level was 95%. Stage-2 Wilson intervals are pointwise across 15 verified decisions without simultaneous multiplicity adjustment. Target attainment status reflects only the specified power/precision targets and does not verify inferential validity (Stage-2 H1 coverage was 100.0% (95% Wilson CI 61.0% to 100.0%); null Type I error was not evaluated). Confirm interval coverage and Type I error in independent simulations at the selected design with validate_design_point(). Each participant is assigned 8 distinct items from a bank of 12 (allocation seed 1). Scope note: This plan uses an exploratory design regime (partial item presentation (items_per_subject = 8) with random slopes) outside the package's pre-benchmarked complete-presentation regimes; confirm empirical interval coverage and null Type I error at the planned sample size via validate_design_point() (and an external estimator when feasible) before pre-registration.
