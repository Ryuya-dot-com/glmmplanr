### Sample Size Justification

No evaluated design has confirmed attainment within constraints. The candidate is 24 participants x 12 items (indeterminate, 6 replications). Joint detection-and-width attainment was 50.0% [18.8%, 81.2%], and mean accuracy difference interval width was 26.27 percentage points (1.331 logit). Final verification covered 15 of 15 lattice points. The same-replication target is 80%. Width limit 30.00 percentage points, width attainment 100.0%, same-replication detection and width attainment 50.0% (MC interval 18.8 to 81.2%). Primary analysis: bayes_laplace, uncorrelated random effects, logit link. Target uncertainty: parametric bootstrap (99 refits per successful replication). The target interval level was 95%. Stage-2 Wilson intervals are pointwise across 15 verified decisions without simultaneous multiplicity adjustment. Target attainment status reflects only the specified power/precision targets and does not verify inferential validity (Stage-2 H1 coverage was 100.0% (95% Wilson CI 61.0% to 100.0%); null Type I error was not evaluated). Confirm interval coverage and Type I error in independent simulations at the selected design with validate_design_point().
